An editorial check in practice

A real summary captured from Talus on September 25, 2026, followed by an illustrative review and suggested edit.

Source

Game Developer reports that Natalie Francis will join Massive Entertainment as managing director on October 1. The article includes her previous roles, leadership comments, and the studio's restructuring.

Read the source article ↗

Live AI output

Former Avalanche Studios chief development officer Natalie Francis is joining Ubisoft subsidiary Massive Entertainment as managing director on October 1st. Francis succeeds Thomas Andren in the leadership role following recent studio restructuring and layoffs. Christoph Hartmann points to her experience managing multidisciplinary teams as preparation for leading the studio behind Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora and Star Wars Outlaws through its next operational chapter.

Review notes

The summary fits the length limit and leads with the appointment. Check names, role, and start date against the source. The leadership endorsement can be cut for a shorter news brief. Spoiler protection does not apply to this news item.

Suggested edit

Natalie Francis will become managing director of Ubisoft's Massive Entertainment on October 1. She previously held leadership roles at Avalanche Studios and DICE. The appointment follows restructuring at the studio.
