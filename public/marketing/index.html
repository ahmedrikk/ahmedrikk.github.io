Case study / AI-assisted editorial

Pixel Pulse

A working gaming news product built to make coverage easier to scan while retaining editorial judgment.

What I built

An AI-assisted editorial product that aggregates gaming coverage from multiple RSS and news sources and transforms it into concise, structured content.

Where AI works

LLM-assisted summarization and content transformation operate under explicit editorial constraints rather than unrestricted generation.

What I owned

Product concept, editorial rules, prompt design, workflow logic, UX decisions, implementation, and the quality-control structure.
